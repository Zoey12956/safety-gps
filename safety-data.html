SAMPLE DATA

Safety evidence in this variant comes only from news reporting and public safety datasets. It deliberately excludes user reviews, ratings and user-submitted reports, which are the evidence source for the comparison variant.

Known coverage gaps

Locations are approximate. Reports are usually tied to a road or an estate rather than a precise point, so markers show an area and never a block or unit number.

Reports are historical. A past incident is not a warning about today, and a single record does not describe the character of a whole neighbourhood.

Under-reporting is expected. Many incidents are never reported or published, so low counts can reflect reporting behaviour rather than a genuinely lower risk.

No reports does not mean the route is safe. When the evidence base for a comparison is too thin, the app shows Insufficient data to compare route safety and makes no recommendation.
